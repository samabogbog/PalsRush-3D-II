## 3. Global Art Direction & Technical Rules

- **Style**: **Tactile Vinyl Chibi Toy-Craft Diorama** (`memory/shared_context.md`). Chunky rounded proportions, soft-touch matte vinyl/clay surfaces (`roughness 0.50`), glossy specular highlights, warm top-left sunlight, and bold **Dark Espresso (`#2B2118`)** outlines.
- **Zero Text Rule**: Strictly **NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, and NO watermarks** inside any image. All titles and badges are rendered by the React HUD layer.
- **Resolutions**:
  - **Landscape Cards (Parts A, B, C)**: `1280 × 720 px` or `1024 × 576 px` (`16:9` aspect ratio, opaque full-bleed diorama scene).
  - **Square Menu & Slot Icons (Part D)**: `512 × 512 px` (`1:1` aspect ratio, centered 85% badge occupancy with transparent corners).
